Damage output by type exploratory

Parsed from action / trait damage rolls of the form N (XdY + Z) <type> damage. Physical vs elemental — damage output exploratory

Total parsed average damage collapsed into Physical (bludgeoning / piercing / slashing), Elemental (acid / cold / fire / lightning / thunder), and Other (force / necrotic / poison / psychic / radiant). A monster can contribute to more than one group. Expand a bar to see contributors.
